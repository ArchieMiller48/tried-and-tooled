## Who this is for

Marketers, small businesses and founders who need a campaign on a calendar, not another list of social tools.

## How to choose

- **The campaign posts most days, or it includes LinkedIn:** Buffer Essentials, priced per channel on [Buffer's pricing page](https://buffer.com/pricing).
- **One brand, about 20 posts in the month, and not LinkedIn or X:** Metricool Free.
- **You only need a caption:** start on [Write posts, emails and landing copy](../../use-cases/write-posts-and-emails/), then come back here to schedule it.

Buffer's pricing page says scheduled posts are unlimited. Its help page says that means a fair-use cap of 5,000 scheduled posts per channel, which is the figure in the avoid note above.
