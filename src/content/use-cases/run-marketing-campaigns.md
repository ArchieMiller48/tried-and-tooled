## Who this is for

Marketers, small businesses and founders who need a campaign on a calendar, not another list of social tools.

## How to choose

- **The campaign posts most days, or it includes LinkedIn:** Buffer Essentials, priced per channel on [Buffer's pricing page](https://buffer.com/pricing).
- **One brand, about 20 posts in the month, and not LinkedIn or X:** Metricool Free.
- **You want a one-off check of whether ChatGPT and Gemini mention the business:** AmIPicked's free check. On 20 Nov 2026 it ran with no sign-up. It does not put posts on a calendar. Buffer Essentials and Metricool Free were not part of that check.
- **You only need a caption:** start on [Write posts, emails and landing copy](../../use-cases/write-posts-and-emails/), then come back here to schedule it.

Buffer's pricing page says scheduled posts are unlimited. Its help page says that means a fair-use cap of 5,000 scheduled posts per channel, which is the figure in the avoid note above.

The note below is a free check of AmIPicked only, dated 20 Nov 2026. No sign-up, no email and no card. Buffer Essentials and Metricool Free were not part of it. The standard task on this page has not been run.

## Hands-on test (20 Nov 2026)

This note is for AmIPicked, at [amipicked.com](https://amipicked.com/). It is a free one-off check of whether AI answers mention a business. It does not schedule posts, and it is not the standard task on this page. Buffer Essentials, the top pick, and Metricool Free, the budget pick, were not part of it. The check ran in the box browser on 20 Nov 2026. No account, no email, no card and no Google sign-in.

The home page says "Free AI visibility checker" and "no signup · results in about a minute". There is one website field and a "Check for free" button. The free check uses ChatGPT and Gemini only.

You enter a URL. AmIPicked reads the site. On these two checks that read took about 4 to 10 seconds. It then shows an editable screen, "Here is what we will ask AI", with the business name, the service, the city and 5 questions. You click "Ask ChatGPT and Gemini". The button's note says "Results in about a minute. No signup." The result appears on the same page.

The first intended site, [colonnadebakery.co.uk](https://www.colonnadebakery.co.uk/), failed in the browser with ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED. curl got a 308 redirect to greggs.com and then failed. bertinet.com only shows a "COMING SOON" page, and thebathbakery.co.uk loads blank. The check used [hobbshousebakery.co.uk](https://www.hobbshousebakery.co.uk/) instead. The site loads. It is an independent family bakery in the Cotswolds and Bristol, not Bath. A Bath site was not confirmed.

The Hobbs House URL was submitted at 11:20:27.6 UTC. The questions screen appeared by 11:20:37. "Ask" was clicked at 11:20:47.98. The result was detected at 11:22:03, polled every few seconds. That is about 75 seconds of asking, and about 96 seconds in total.

The five questions were:

1. "Can you recommend a good family-run bakery that delivers organic bread across the UK?"
2. "What is the best place to buy sourdough bread online in the UK?"
3. "I need a reliable supplier for handmade sandwich loaves for my small café, which bakery should I look at?"
4. "Which UK bakery sells baking equipment and bread boxes as gifts, and is it worth the price?"
5. "How does a bakery that supplies both wholesale and home customers compare to other UK artisan bakers for quality bread?"

ChatGPT and Gemini were the engines. Gemini returned "This model did not answer. Try again in a minute." on question 1.

The result was 3/9. The page said "AI answers mention your business in 3 of 5 questions" and "AI recommends you only sometimes". Question 1: ChatGPT did not mention Hobbs House, and Gemini did not answer. Question 2: ChatGPT did not mention it, and Gemini recommended it at #2. Question 3: ChatGPT recommended it at #1, and Gemini did not mention it. Question 4: ChatGPT did not mention it, and Gemini recommended it at #1. Question 5: neither engine mentioned it.

Hobbs House itself was 33% (avg #1.3). Other businesses named, and their share of answers: E5 Bakehouse 33% (avg #2), Bread Ahead 33% (avg #2), Lovingly Artisan 22%, Botham's of Whitby 22%, and The Authentic Bread Company 11%.

Cited sources on that result included hobbshousebakery.co.uk (3), e5bakehouse.com (3), abelandcole.co.uk (3), waitrose.com (2), lovinglyartisan.com (2), breadahead.com (2), botham.co.uk (2) and rekki.com (2). The full answers also contained inline URLs such as happerley.co.uk, honeyrosebakery.com, waldrons.com and thesourdoughschool.com.

Two citations were opened. The ChatGPT question 3 citation, hobbshousebakery.co.uk/pages/wholesale, was claimed to show Hobbs House supplying artisan, tin-baked sandwich loaves to cafés and wholesale customers. The page exists. It is a wholesale page about direct deliveries and distributors. It mentions "artisan" but has no "sandwich" or "café" text, so it only partly supports the claim. The ChatGPT question 3 citation, e5bakehouse.com/wholesale, was claimed to show cycle delivery to local cafés in Hackney. The page says "We deliver bread to local cafes, shops and restaurants by bicycle", so it supports the claim.

The second check was Greggs. The URL was submitted at 11:22:49.3 UTC. The questions screen appeared at 11:22:53. "Ask" was clicked at 11:22:58.3. The result was detected by 11:23:39.5. That is about 41 seconds of asking, and about 50 seconds in total.

The five questions were:

1. "Can you recommend a good bakery near me for breakfast on the go?"
2. "What's the best place in the UK for cheap sausage rolls and sandwiches at lunchtime?"
3. "Where can I order bakery food for delivery to my home in the UK?"
4. "Which high street bakery chain has the best vegan or vegetarian savoury bakes?"
5. "Is there a way to get a discount or free treat on my lunch purchases in the UK?"

ChatGPT and Gemini both answered every question. The result was 8/10. The page said the business was mentioned in 5 of 5 questions, and it still said "AI recommends you only sometimes". Question 1: ChatGPT did not mention Greggs, and Gemini recommended it at #2. Question 2: both recommended it at #1. Question 3: ChatGPT recommended it at #1, and Gemini did not mention it. Question 4: both recommended it at #1. Question 5: ChatGPT recommended it at #6, and Gemini recommended it at #1.

Greggs itself was 80% (avg #1.8). Other businesses named, and their share of answers: Tesco 40%, GAIL's Bakery 40%, Boots 30%, Sainsbury's 30%, and Cooplands 30%.

Cited sources on that result included gails.com (3), greggsmenusprices.co.uk (3), greggs.com (2), coop.co.uk (2), bakeryinfo.co.uk (2), mealdealer.uk (2), ubereats.com (2) and pret.co.uk (2).

Two citations were opened, and both supported the claim. A Guardian sausage-roll article was cited for a classic sausage roll at about £1.35. The headline and text say a sausage roll now costs £1.35 in most shops. greggs.com/order was cited for delivery via Just Eat and Uber Eats. The page says Greggs has teamed up with Just Eat and Uber Eats to deliver.

The checker reported "AI search bots have access", "No llms.txt file" and "No structured data" for greggs.com. For Hobbs House it reported all three present. This check did not open an llms.txt file or look for structured data, so those lines were not checked independently.

No "checks left" counter or gate appeared on either check. The upsells seen were a "Remind me in 30 days" email option, which was not used, a "Download PDF" button, which was not clicked, and the line "Five questions are a sample. Track it daily and see what works."

Prices on [AmIPicked's pricing page](https://amipicked.com/pricing/), read on 20 Nov 2026: the free check is $0, one-off, no card, 5 questions, ChatGPT and Gemini, and the page says no signup and no card. Starter is $29/month with a 7-day free trial and tracks 10 prompts daily. Growth is $59/month with a 7-day free trial and tracks 25 prompts daily, for up to 3 businesses. Both paid plans say the card is charged after 7 days. A one-off report is $39 for 20 questions in 4 models. It is credited to a plan if you start tracking within 30 days. The yearly toggle says "2 months free".

Paid tracking, the PDF download and the email reminder were not tested. The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is the same 12-post, two-week campaign to one Instagram account and one Facebook page in each tool, including one AI-drafted caption per tool.
