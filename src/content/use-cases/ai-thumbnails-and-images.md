## How to choose

- **The text has to be right:** Ideogram. The comparison is [Ideogram vs Midjourney](../../compare/ideogram-vs-midjourney/). Prices on that page were checked 17 Nov 2026. It does not change the picks above.
- **You want the most striking art for the least money:** Midjourney Basic, then add the title in Ideogram or in a design tool if the letters are wrong. [Midjourney's text doc](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32502277092109-Text-Generation), read on 17 Nov 2026, says versions 6 and later can put words inside double quotation marks, and that shorter phrases work better. That is the vendor's description. Nothing was generated.
- **You want the shortlist, including a YouTube thumbnail tool:** [Best AI thumbnail generators](../../best/ai-thumbnail-makers/). Prices on that page were checked 17 Nov 2026.
