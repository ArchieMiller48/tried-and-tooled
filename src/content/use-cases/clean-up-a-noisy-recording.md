## Who this is for

Creators and anyone else who has a voice note or a podcast guest recorded in a noisy room, and needs a cleaner file before the edit.

## How to choose

- **One voice file, under 30 minutes:** Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech on the free plan. Limits are on [Adobe's features page](https://podcast.adobe.com/en/features). No Premium dollar amount is printed there.
- **A draft of up to 2 hours, and a jingle is fine:** Auphonic Free, on [Auphonic's pricing page](https://auphonic.com/pricing).
- **The file is a voiceover you still have to generate:** [AI voiceover for videos](../../use-cases/ai-voiceover/).
- **The job is a transcript, not a cleaner file:** [Transcribe meetings and interviews](../../use-cases/transcribe-meetings-and-interviews/).
