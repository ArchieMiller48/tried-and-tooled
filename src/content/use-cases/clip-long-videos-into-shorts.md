## Hands-on test (27 Nov 2026)

This note is for OpusClip, the top pick. The check ran on 27 Nov 2026 at [clip.opus.pro](https://clip.opus.pro), on a new account. Descript (Hobbyist), the budget pick, was not part of it.

Sign-in was by email with a 6-digit code. There was no password, no phone and no card. A new account starts a 7-day free trial: 90 credits, no card, and no charge unless you upgrade. This check ran on that trial, not on the plain Free plan.

The file was a 25-minute, two-person TV interview: CAN TV Political Forum with Chicago Alderman George Cardenas, CC BY 3.0, from Wikimedia Commons. The standard task on this page is a 30-minute interview. This file is close to that task. It is not exactly that task.

Upload of the local file took about 6 seconds. Processing cost 25 credits, at 1 credit per minute of video. Clipping, captions and the auto headline were free. The request was for 9:16 clips under 60 seconds. The length options were under 30 seconds and 30 to 59 seconds. Processing took about 8 to 9 minutes.

The result was 28 clips. The virality scores ran from 99 to 79. The top 5 were 30 to 53 seconds long. Each covered a different topic from the interview: immigration and foster care, mentoring students, recycling, donated bikes, and school closures. Two of the top 5 stitched together non-adjacent parts of the interview.

The top clip exported in about 1 minute. The file was a 1080×1920 MP4, 36.7 seconds long. It carried an OpusClip logo watermark in the top-left. The download popup said "Trial and free users will see OpusClip logo in their clips". The burned-in captions were word-by-word. They matched a separate speech-to-text reference, with no clear errors. Filler words such as "uh" were dropped. The speaker's face stayed centred in the vertical frame. The auto headline was accurate. The top-ranked clip ended mid-word.

Subtitle and transcript downloads, and Premiere and DaVinci export, are listed as Pro features on the upgrade page. They were not used.

Free, on the pricing page read on 27 Nov 2026, is $0, with 60 credits a month, a watermark, no editing, clips that can't be exported after 3 days, the slowest queue, and local upload up to 10 GB. Starter is $15/mo, with 150 credits, and it removes the watermark. Pro is $29/mo, or $14.50/mo billed yearly. Those Free limits were read on the pricing page. The plain Free plan was not used.

Not tested: the plain Free plan after the trial, the editor, exporting the other clips, YouTube link import, and reframing when the interviewer speaks. Descript (Hobbyist) stays pending.

The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is the same 30-minute interview in each tool, 5 vertical clips under 60 seconds, scored on clip selection, caption accuracy, reframing and total hands-on time.
