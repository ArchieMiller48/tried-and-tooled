## Who this is for

Founders, creators and small teams who write the same kinds of posts and emails every week and want a first draft, not a ghostwriter they never check.

## How to choose

- **You write for clients most days:** Claude Pro, with the voice guide in a project.
- **You need a draft and you are not paying yet:** ChatGPT Free's everyday text chats.
- **The same story has to become a deck:** [Make a slide deck](../../use-cases/make-a-slide-deck/) covers Gamma and Claude Slides. ChatGPT Slides is not on the free plan.

The note below is a signed-out check of nixm only, dated 8 Nov 2026. Claude and ChatGPT were not part of it. The standard task on this page has not been run.

## Hands-on test (8 Nov 2026)

This note is for nixm, on [nixm.ai](https://nixm.ai/). It was signed out. No account, no email, no password, no card and no Google sign-in. The Google Analytics cookie was declined. Claude and ChatGPT were not part of it.

The brief was a customer email for Hollow Oak Bakery, an independent bakery in Bath, founded in 2026. The products named were sourdough loaves, croissants and cinnamon buns. A weekend pastry box was £18, to collect in store on Saturday from 9am to 11am, with no delivery. The ask was about 120 words. The brief said not to invent awards, a street address, extra prices, a website, opening hours other than that Saturday window, delivery or extra products, and not to use revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, synergy or world-class.

The brief was sent at about 11:11:36 UTC. The first complete reply was at about 11:12:04 UTC, about 28 seconds later. The times are from clock readings and are approximate to a few seconds. The address bar stayed at https://nixm.ai/. No shareable signal URL or NIXM id appeared. No chat credit counter or charge was shown.

The draft, counted from the subject line through the signoff and excluding the wrapper and the follow-up offer, was 112 words. It kept the bakery name, Bath, the three named products, £18, and in-store Saturday 9am–11am collection. No awards, street address, extra prices, website, delivery or extra products appeared. None of the forbidden words appeared. It implied recurring weekly availability with "every saturday", although the brief only gave a Saturday collection window, and it added "we bake everything ourselves", which the brief did not state in those words. "beautiful box" and "the hollow oak bakery team" are extra colour.

The reply opened with "here's the email:" and closed with "clean, honest, no fluff. want a subject line variant or a shorter social caption version?" The email as displayed was:

> subject: introducing our weekend pastry box 🥐
> hi there,
> we have something we're really excited to share with you.
> starting this weekend, hollow oak bakery is offering a weekend pastry box — a handpicked selection of our sourdough loaves, croissants and cinnamon buns, brought together in one beautiful box for £18.
> collection is in store every saturday between 9am and 11am. that's it — no fuss, just fresh pastries waiting for you.
> we bake everything ourselves, with care, right here in bath. this box is our way of making it a little easier to bring something special to your weekend table.
> we'd love to see you on saturday.
> with warmth,
> the hollow oak bakery team

In the same session, a still of a wooden bakery shop sign reading "Hollow Oak Bakery" was sent at about 11:12:33 UTC. About 34 seconds later the page said "signing an account is needed to generate images — sign up and it fires the moment you're in." A banner read "[ SIGNUP FREE & LOGIN TO MAKE IMAGES — 20 CREDITS ON US ]" with a SIGN UP TO RENDER button. No image was generated. No video was started. Signup was not completed. Other signup copy seen: "20 free credits to start", "your first video, free with a code", and "image & video generation features." No plan prices or payment details appeared.

Vendor pages claim logged-out signals are public by link and dissolve after seven days, and some copy says a still can be made without an account. In this check, signed-out chat worked and the still was gated. A shareable NIXM link was not seen. Privacy, transcripts and dissolution were not verified.

Magic-link signup, the stated 20-credit grant, video, web research and signed-in stills were not tested. The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is a 500-word blog post and a 120-word customer email in Claude and in ChatGPT, scored on the voice guide, invented facts, and edits a person still has to make.
