## Who this is for

Students and researchers who have been asked whether a draft looks generated, and who need the limit in writing before they paste a long piece.

## How to choose

- **You want the check that Grammarly lists on Pro:** the plan data is $30 USD for 1 month, $60 USD for 3 months and $144 USD for 1 year. The visible card says try Pro for $0.00. See [Grammarly's plans](https://www.grammarly.com/plans).
- **One essay, and 10,000 words a month is enough:** GPTZero Free, from the FAQ on [GPTZero's pricing page](https://gptzero.me/pricing). A paid dollar amount was not in that HTML.
- **The job is grammar and a rewrite, not a detector score:** [Proofread and edit a piece of writing](../../use-cases/proofread-and-edit/).
- **The file is an image, not a paragraph:** [Check if an image is AI](../../use-cases/check-if-an-image-is-ai/).

The note below is a signed-out check of GPTZero Free, dated 24 Nov 2026. Grammarly Pro and Grammarly Free were not part of it. Prices were read on 23 Nov 2026. The standard task on this page has not been run.

Grammarly's page says it is trusted by over 40 million people. That is the company's own figure, not a result from this site. GPTZero's page does not print a paid price in the HTML read today.

## Hands-on test (24 Nov 2026)

This note is for GPTZero Free, the budget pick. It was signed out, with no account. Grammarly Pro and Grammarly Free were not part of it.

The paste box on [gptzero.me](https://gptzero.me/), then Scan, opened app.gptzero.me. Signed out throughout. The result label was "GPTZero AI Detection, Model 5.0b".

Three texts were scanned.

A 2018 Wikipedia passage, "Sally Lunn bun", 256 words, was labelled "We are highly confident this text is entirely human", at 0% AI and 100% human. That result took about 2.8 seconds.

An AI-written text of 190 words was labelled "highly confident this text was AI generated", at 100% AI. That result took about 8.8 seconds.

A mix of two human paragraphs and one AI paragraph was labelled "a mix of AI and human". 3 of 8 sentences were likely AI, and only the AI paragraph was highlighted. That result took about 1.0 seconds.

The first scan triggered a browser check and an image captcha.

Signed out, the input cap is 10,000 characters, with a 250-character minimum. The page showed "10,000 credits left this month". Credits are per word. "See sentence highlighting" on the human result opened a "Sign up to Continue" modal. It did not block scans. Advanced scan showed "1/1 free scans left" and was not used. A Premium popup showed £9.09 a month, billed annually, first year 30% off, was £12.99.

Not tested: account features, Advanced scan, plagiarism, file upload, long or non-English text, paraphrased AI text, and more human samples. One human sample only, so false positives on more human writing were not tested.

The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is the same 600-word draft, with a known human paragraph and a known generated paragraph, through each tool.
