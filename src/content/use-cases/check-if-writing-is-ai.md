## Who this is for

Students and researchers who have been asked whether a draft looks generated, and who need the limit in writing before they paste a long piece.

## How to choose

- **You want the check that Grammarly lists on Pro:** the plan data is $30 USD for 1 month, $60 USD for 3 months and $144 USD for 1 year. The visible card says try Pro for $0.00. See [Grammarly's plans](https://www.grammarly.com/plans).
- **One essay, and 10,000 words a month is enough:** GPTZero Free, from the FAQ on [GPTZero's pricing page](https://gptzero.me/pricing). A paid dollar amount was not in that HTML.
- **The job is grammar and a rewrite, not a detector score:** [Proofread and edit a piece of writing](../../use-cases/proofread-and-edit/).
- **The file is an image, not a paragraph:** [Check if an image is AI](../../use-cases/check-if-an-image-is-ai/).
