## Who this is for

YouTubers, short-form creators and founders who need a few striking shots for a video, ad or launch post, and don't have a camera crew.

## How to choose

- **You want to try several models in one place:** Runway.
- **You're on £0:** Google Flow's daily credits, used at off-peak times.
- **You need long, consistent shots of one character or product:** Kling 4.0 is on Runway's cards until the 30 November discount, and you can still test it directly.
- **You want a 15-second clip from the Gemini API:** Omni 1.2 Flash is in the also-considered list. It is not the top pick.
