## Who this is for

YouTubers, podcasters and small businesses who need intro music, background beds or a jingle, and want to avoid copyright claims.

## How to choose

- **Music matters to the content:** Suno Pro.
- **You already pay for ElevenLabs:** use Eleven Music from the same credits.
- **You only need a few seconds of sting or ambience:** try your existing tools first before you add another subscription.
