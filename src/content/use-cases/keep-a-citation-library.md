## Who this is for

Students and researchers who need a bibliography they can reuse, not a one-off summary of a paper.

## How to choose

- **You need the citation in the document:** Zotero, with the word-processor plugin described in the [documentation](https://www.zotero.org/support/).
- **You are only storing references, not a pile of PDFs:** the free 300 MB row on the [storage page](https://www.zotero.org/storage).
- **You still have to find the papers:** [Research and summarise sources](../../use-cases/research-and-summarise-sources/) is the search. This page is the library afterwards.
- **The essay still has to be edited:** [Proofread and edit a piece of writing](../../use-cases/proofread-and-edit/).
