## Who this is for

Designers, small businesses and anyone else who needs a logo and a few graphics that match it, not a team design system.

## How to choose

- **You will keep making posts, decks and other layouts:** Canva Pro. The US price is on [Canva's plan comparison](https://www.canva.com/learn/canva-pro-vs-canva-enterprise/).
- **You want the logo plus a set of branded templates, and a lower yearly price than Canva Pro:** Looka Brand Kit at $96/year on [Looka's help page](https://help.looka.com/en/articles/4636515-what-is-the-brand-kit).
- **The graphic is a YouTube thumbnail with a title on it:** use [AI thumbnails and social images](../../use-cases/ai-thumbnails-and-images/) instead of a logo tool.
- **The job is a product cutout, not a logo:** [Cut out a product photo](../../use-cases/cut-out-a-product-photo/).
- **The job is one app screen:** [Design an app screen](../../use-cases/design-an-app-screen/).

Prices above are the figures those pages showed when checked. Canva says its guide is US pricing and that other regions should use the [pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/).

The note below is a signed-out check of Looka's free logo maker, dated 5 Nov 2026. It records the checkout prices. It does not include a paid Brand Kit, a downloaded file or a 1080×1080 social graphic. Canva Pro was not part of it.

## Hands-on test (5 Nov 2026)

This note is for Looka Brand Kit, the budget pick, and only for the free path on the [logo maker](https://looka.com/logo-maker/). It was signed out, with no account, no card and no payment. Canva Pro was not part of it. The paid Brand Kit was not bought.

The brief was the company name Hollow Oak Bakery, the industry Bakery, and the slogan Baked in Bath since 2026. Looka did not ask for a tone. The colour step offered 9 swatches: Blue, Purple, Pink, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal and Greyscale. There was no brown and no cream, so Orange and Yellow were selected. Bakery, Cake and Pie were selected on the symbol step, which also listed Cupcake. The top bar later showed Symbols 0, so those choices may not have registered. One inspiration logo, the Farmpack tile, was selected. The name step said logos look better with shorter company names, and suggested shortening the name or moving some of it to the slogan.

No email or account was asked for before the logos appeared. The first logo appeared about 15 to 20 seconds after the brief was submitted. The clock readings are about five seconds either way. About 23 logos were on the page, with a Load more logos button. After the results loaded, the menu showed a guest-style user name. This check did not type it and did not submit a sign-up. Sign-up modals were in the page markup and were not displayed.

The first 6 logos all spelled Hollow Oak Bakery correctly. No garbled or misspelt name was seen. Two of those six were in capitals. One placed a symbol between HOLLOW OAK and BAKERY. One used wide letter spacing. Where the slogan was inspected, it read BAKED IN BATH SINCE 2026. The colours were mostly navy, orange, gold, cream and peach, rather than a brown or cream rustic set. The first result was a navy hexagon with orange text. The early symbols were abstract: a hexagon, a flame and a pen-nib, rather than bakery symbols.

The editor opened signed out. The left menu included Layout, Palette, Background, Name, Slogan, Symbol and Container. The palette showed recolour variants, including lilac, green, cream and navy, without a sign-in. An edit was not saved, and fonts were not tried. The preview showed foil on a card, and a phone social profile. Brand Essentials listed Social Profiles 20, Social Covers 55, Business Cards 19, Email Signatures 8, Letterheads 10, Brand Guidelines 10, and a website. Digital Marketing Essentials listed Social Posts 57, Social Stories 10, Advertisements 63 and Blog Banners 11. The page said that 100+ branded and customizable designs are included in the Brand Kit subscription. Those designs were not generated. A 1080×1080 size label was not looked for, and a social graphic of that size was not made.

Generating, browsing, previewing, editing and the mockup previews did not ask for payment. Every download is paid. No account was required to reach the checkout page. The prices were in US dollars, and no pound price was shown. Basic Logo Package was $20 as a one-time purchase, for one low-res file and no transparent file. Premium Logo Package was $65 as a one-time purchase. Its card listed multiple high-res file types, unlimited changes, lifetime technical support and full ownership. Brand Kit Subscription, marked Most Popular, was $96 a year, billed annually. The page showed a previous price of $192 and said save 50%. The card listed 300+ branded assets, business cards, email signatures, social profiles and covers, letterheads, and the line "Over $3,000 in Exclusive Offers". Brand Kit Web Subscription was $129 a year, billed annually. The page showed a previous price of $215 and said save 40%. That plan listed everything in Brand Kit plus a multipage website, described on the card as AI generated. Select was not clicked. No file was downloaded, and no template was generated from a paid Brand Kit.

The standard task on this page, the same one-paragraph brief as a logo and a 1080×1080 social graphic in each tool, has not been run.
