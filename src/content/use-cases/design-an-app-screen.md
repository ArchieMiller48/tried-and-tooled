## Who this is for

Designers, founders and students who need a picture of one screen they can edit, not a hosted app and not a logo.

## How to choose

- **You will share the file and keep editing it:** Figma Professional.
- **You are trying one draft:** Figma Starter, and watch the 500 credit monthly cap.
- **The screen has to become a working site:** [Vibe-code an MVP](../../use-cases/vibe-code-an-mvp/) covers Lovable and Bolt. v0 is listed above as a coded alternative, not the layout pick.
- **You want a prompt to sketch one screen before you have a file:** Dessn, on the Free plan checked on 9 Nov 2026. It started from a blank page, with no repository connected. The screen included placeholder copy that was not in the brief, and no export or download control was seen.
- **You needed a logo or a thumbnail, not a screen:** [Design logos and graphics](../../use-cases/design-logos-and-graphics/) and [AI thumbnails and social images](../../use-cases/ai-thumbnails-and-images/).

The note below is a check of Dessn Free only, dated 9 Nov 2026. Figma and v0 were not part of it. The standard task on this page has not been run.

## Hands-on test (9 Nov 2026)

This note is for Dessn, on the Free plan, at [create.dessn.ai](https://create.dessn.ai/). Sign-in was a one-time email link. The page showed a "Sign in as…" button and no password. No card was asked for. The code-entry screen was not showing when the browser started, so this check used the link. Figma and v0 were not part of it.

No starter or example was offered. Dessn said a blank page was available, and that code could be connected later: "Yes: you can start from a blank page and design from scratch. You can connect your code later." This check started from a blank page and did not connect a repository.

The prompt was: "Design a mobile order screen for Hollow Oak Bakery, a small UK bakery: list 3 products (Sourdough loaf £4.50, Cinnamon bun £2.80, Seeded rye £4.20) with quantity steppers, a Saturday 9-11am collection time picker, an order total, and a big Place order button. Warm brown and cream colours."

The prompt was submitted at 11:13:50.109 UTC on 9 Nov 2026. The first designed frame was visible by 11:15:56 UTC, about 2 minutes 6 seconds later. The time is approximate, from the browser clock and the screenshot. The preview was named "Hollow Oak Bakery order screen". Dessn reported the route as /order.

The screen showed all three product names and prices exactly: Sourdough loaf £4.50, Cinnamon bun £2.80 and Seeded rye £4.20. Quantity steppers were present and working. The starting quantities were 1 sourdough loaf, 2 cinnamon buns and 0 seeded rye. The total was £10.10, which matches those quantities. The collection picker was labelled Saturday, 9–11am, with slots at 9:00, 9:30, 10:00 and 10:30. 9:30 was selected. The colours were warm brown, cream and terracotta. No spelling error was seen.

Copy that was not in the brief was also on the screen: "Baked this morning on Mill Lane", made-up product descriptions, and "Collect from the counter within 30 minutes of your slot." Those lines are placeholders. They would need an edit before the screen could be shared as the bakery's own text.

During generation, one step showed "1 action failed" and "This step could not finish". Dessn continued on its own, and the screen then rendered.

After the design, the account showed 20 credits remaining. Dessn said "This first one is on us." The balance before the prompt was not recorded, so the credits used on this screen were not confirmed. The pricing page, re-read on 9 Nov 2026, lists Free at $0 per month with 20 monthly credits, Pro at $30 per editor per month with 100 monthly credits, and Enterprise as custom.

A Share panel was on the screen. It showed "Copy link", "Invite", access "Anyone in mail", "Editors can edit · viewers only view", and "Get preview link", which stayed disabled until a frame on the board was selected. No Free-plan paywall wording was in that panel. No export or download control was seen. The share controls were present. This check does not show that a public preview link or a download works on Free.

Figma and v0 were not part of this check. The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is one mobile screen with a title, a price and a primary button in Figma and in v0, scored on whether the text is readable, whether the price matches the brief, and how many edits it takes before the file can be shared.
