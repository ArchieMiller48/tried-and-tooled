## Who this is for

A shop or a solo service that gets the same questions on the site and does not want a full support suite.

## How to choose

- **Questions arrive most days and the answer is already on your site:** Tidio Lyro, from $32.50/mo on [Tidio's pricing page](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/).
- **You can still reply yourself:** Tidio Free, $0/mo, 50 billable conversations.
- **You are comparing a human inbox:** Crisp's table starts at $0, then $45 per month per workspace. This page did not re-check which AI features sit on that $0 plan.

Tidio says its customers average a 75% resolution rate with Lyro. That figure is the company's own, from the pricing page, and it is not a test run for this site.
