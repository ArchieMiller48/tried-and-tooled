## Who this is for

Students, researchers and anyone else who has a draft and needs the mistakes caught before it goes to a reader. The draft is still yours to check.

## How to choose

- **You want full-sentence rewrites and a tone control:** Grammarly Pro. The visible card says try Pro for $0.00. Plan data in the page lists $30 USD for 1 month, $60 USD for 3 months and $144 USD for 1 year. Free is $0 USD/month. See [Grammarly's plans](https://www.grammarly.com/plans).
- **A first pass, and the section is under 2,000 characters:** LanguageTool Basic, free on [LanguageTool's pricing table](https://languagetool.org/pricing).
- **You need a full-sentence rewrite and you are signed out of Grammarly:** on 19 Nov 2026 the [grammar-check page](https://www.grammarly.com/grammar-check), with no account, showed the first spelling fix and then a sign-up modal. A 618-word post was cut to 709 characters with no warning. No rewrite option appeared. Grammarly Pro was not part of that check.
- **You want the two plans side by side:** [Grammarly vs LanguageTool](../../compare/grammarly-vs-languagetool/). Prices on that page were checked 16 Nov 2026. It does not change the picks above.
- **The piece is a new draft, not an edit:** start on [Write posts, emails and landing copy](../../use-cases/write-posts-and-emails/), then come back here.

## Hands-on test (4 Nov 2026)

This note is for LanguageTool Basic, the budget pick, on the [homepage editor](https://languagetool.org/), default English, signed out. No account, no card and no Google sign-in. Grammarly Pro and Grammarly Free were not part of it.

The text was typed, not pasted. It was a short business email with 8 planted errors, plus a lowercase friday: "Hi Sarah, thank's for you're email yesterday. Me and the team has reviewed the proposal and we think its a strong peice of work. However, their are a few points we would like to discus before friday. Could you send the revised figures by tomorow?"

8 flags were showing as typing finished, while "Checking text..." was still on screen. The full list, 10 flags, appeared within a few seconds. The exact time was not measured.

All 8 planted errors were caught at least partly. thank's was marked as thanks, you're as your, its as it's, peice as piece, their as there, discus as discuss, and tomorow as tomorrow. In "Me and the team has", Me was flagged, with a suggestion of I and a note to put yourself second. The has subject-verb error was not flagged, and the editor did not suggest "The team and I have". That one counts as partial. The lowercase friday was flagged as a spelling mistake, with Friday as the first suggestion.

One arguable false positive: a suggested comma before and, joining two independent clauses.

The counter read "Characters 246/2000", the free cap of 2,000 characters per check. The page also showed "Paraphrasing 0/3". The paraphrase tab was not used. No Premium locks were seen in this check, and there was no hidden premium-suggestion count. Picky Mode was not opened. The only premium prompt was the promo text "Unlock all Premium Features". Sign-up was not needed.

A longer document was not tested. The standard task on this page, the same 600-word draft with 15 planted grammar errors and 3 planted factual claims in each tool, has not been run. A signed-out Grammarly Free check, dated 19 Nov 2026, is in the next section. It is not that standard task.

## Hands-on test (19 Nov 2026)

This note is for Grammarly Free, the avoid pick, when the job is a full-sentence rewrite. The check ran on 19 Nov 2026 at about 11:22 GMT, on the [grammar-check page](https://www.grammarly.com/grammar-check). It was signed out, with no account, in a desktop browser. Grammarly Pro and LanguageTool were not part of it.

The first text was a short email of 247 characters with 8 planted errors. 9 underlines appeared in about 5 seconds. Signed out, only the first suggestion could be read. thank's was marked as thanks, with the label "Fix spelling". Hovering or clicking any other underline opened a sign-up modal that said "Get Grammarly for free to get more". "Me and the team has" was not flagged.

A blog post of 618 words (3,355 characters) was silently cut to 709 characters. That is about 133 words, about 21% of the post. There was no warning. Only 4 suggestions were shown for that part.

No rewrite, rephrase or tone option appeared signed out. The page says generative AI, tone and clarity suggestions need an account or Pro.

Not tested: a free Grammarly account (signed in), the browser extension, the desktop app, Pro, and the paraphrasing tool.

Tested 19 Nov 2026: Grammarly Free, signed out, showed only the first fix, silently cut a post to 709 characters, and offered no rewrite. Detection was fast. Signed out, only the first fix could be read, there was a silent cap of about 709 characters, and there was no free rewrite. That supports this avoid pick when the job is a full-sentence rewrite.

The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is the same 600-word draft, with 15 planted grammar errors and 3 planted factual claims, through each tool.
