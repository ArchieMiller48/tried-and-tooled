## Who this is for

Students, researchers and anyone else who has a draft and needs the mistakes caught before it goes to a reader. The draft is still yours to check.

## How to choose

- **You want full-sentence rewrites and a tone control:** Grammarly Pro. The plans page lists Pro at $12 USD and does not label that figure per month. Free is $0 USD/month. See [Grammarly's plans](https://www.grammarly.com/plans).
- **A first pass, and the section is under 2,000 characters:** LanguageTool Basic, free on [LanguageTool's pricing table](https://languagetool.org/pricing).
- **The piece is a new draft, not an edit:** start on [Write posts, emails and landing copy](../../use-cases/write-posts-and-emails/), then come back here.

The note below is a signed-out check of LanguageTool Basic, dated 4 Nov 2026. Grammarly Pro and Grammarly Free were not part of it.

Grammarly's page says it is trusted by over 40 million people. That is the company's own figure, not a result from this site.

## Hands-on test (4 Nov 2026)

This note is for LanguageTool Basic, the budget pick, on the [homepage editor](https://languagetool.org/), default English, signed out. No account, no card and no Google sign-in. Grammarly Pro and Grammarly Free were not part of it.

The text was typed, not pasted. It was a short business email with 8 planted errors, plus a lowercase friday: "Hi Sarah, thank's for you're email yesterday. Me and the team has reviewed the proposal and we think its a strong peice of work. However, their are a few points we would like to discus before friday. Could you send the revised figures by tomorow?"

8 flags were showing as typing finished, while "Checking text..." was still on screen. The full list, 10 flags, appeared within a few seconds. The exact time was not measured.

All 8 planted errors were caught at least partly. thank's was marked as thanks, you're as your, its as it's, peice as piece, their as there, discus as discuss, and tomorow as tomorrow. In "Me and the team has", Me was flagged, with a suggestion of I and a note to put yourself second. The has subject-verb error was not flagged, and the editor did not suggest "The team and I have". That one counts as partial. The lowercase friday was flagged as a spelling mistake, with Friday as the first suggestion.

One arguable false positive: a suggested comma before and, joining two independent clauses.

The counter read "Characters 246/2000", the free cap of 2,000 characters per check. The page also showed "Paraphrasing 0/3". The paraphrase tab was not used. No Premium locks were seen in this check, and there was no hidden premium-suggestion count. Picky Mode was not opened. The only premium prompt was the promo text "Unlock all Premium Features". Sign-up was not needed.

A longer document was not tested. The standard task on this page, the same 600-word draft with 15 planted grammar errors and 3 planted factual claims in each tool, has not been run.
