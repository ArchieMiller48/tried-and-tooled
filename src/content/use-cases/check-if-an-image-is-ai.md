## Who this is for

Students, researchers and anyone else who has a photo or a piece of artwork and needs a check on whether it was made or edited with AI. The verdict is something to read next to the file. It is not a final ruling.

## How to choose

- **You want a free check and you do not want an account:** Phrasly AI Image Detector. The [detector page](https://phrasly.ai/ai-image-detector) says the check is free forever, with no account, no sign-up and no credit card. It lists JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF and GIF, up to 20 MB, and says unusually heavy use from one browser or network is paused for a while. This check did not hit that pause.
- **You want the free path, which is the same page:** there is no cheaper image-check plan on that page. On 6 Nov 2026 [Phrasly's pricing page](https://phrasly.ai/pricing) showed writing plans, 3 Day Access at $2.00 and Unlimited at $10.99 per month billed annually, with the annual view selected. Those plans were not bought. This note does not say what they add to an image check.
- **You were going to decide by odd hands or garbled text:** Phrasly's guide on the detector page says looking for odd hands or garbled text no longer works on new models, and that a careful look is no longer enough.
- **You are making the picture, not checking one:** use [AI thumbnails and social images](../../use-cases/ai-thumbnails-and-images/) or [Design a logo and graphics](../../use-cases/design-logos-and-graphics/).

The note below is a signed-out check of Phrasly only, dated 6 Nov 2026. It is five images: two real photos, two AI JPEGs, and one example tile on the site. Phrasly's page says its in-house model is over 99% accurate in Phrasly's own testing, and 100% certain when a SynthID watermark or a verified AI signature is found. Those figures are the vendor's claims. This check did not measure them.

## Hands-on test (6 Nov 2026)

This note is for Phrasly AI Image Detector, on the [detector page](https://phrasly.ai/ai-image-detector). It was signed out. No account, no password, no card and no Google sign-in. The cookie banner was declined. No other detector was part of it. The four files went through the upload box. The example was a click on a tile already on the page.

Two Unsplash camera photos, not AI images, were uploaded first. `real-photo-1.jpg` is a mountain landscape JPEG at 800×533. The UI said Likely real, with the note "Our model found no sign of AI generation." Confidence was 96%. AI signature was None found. File metadata was No AI tags. Pixel detection was No AI found. There was no SynthID row in the final panel. It was about 8 to 10 seconds. `real-photo-2.jpg` is a dog JPEG at 800×1067. It had the same Likely real note, at 97%, with the same signature, metadata, pixel and SynthID lines. It was under about 12 seconds.

Two AI-generated JPEGs, made for this test, were uploaded next. Both are 1024×1024. `ai-image-1-bakery.jpg` is a Hollow Oak Bakery wooden sign. `ai-image-2-dog.jpg` is a golden retriever with sunglasses on a beach. Both read AI-generated at 97%, with the note "The file carries a signature saying it was made with AI." AI signature was flagged as AI-made. File metadata was No AI tags. Pixel detection was Not needed, and it was skipped. There was no SynthID row. Both were under about 12 seconds. The detector page says a verified signature, or a SynthID watermark, makes the verdict 100% certain. That is the vendor's claim. These two files showed 97%, a flagged AI signature and no SynthID row. This check does not say whether that signature was verified.

The site's ChatGPT example tile, a ramen preview, was used once after those four uploads. It read AI-generated at 100%, with the note "Made with OpenAI. It carries a SynthID watermark and OpenAI's signature." AI signature was OpenAI, flagged. File metadata was No AI tags. Pixel detection was Not needed. SynthID was Found · OpenAI. It was about 2 seconds after the click.

The times are approximate. For the uploads, the result was already showing when it was first checked, around 10 to 12 seconds. Across the four files, that is roughly 8 to 12 seconds. The example was about 2 seconds.

Every result had an Original / Heatmap toggle and a slider. Heatmap was the default view and showed a red tint on the AI images.

The two AI JPEGs were caught by the embedded signature check, not by pixel detection. Pixel detection was skipped once a signature was found. They are not a clean case of a watermark stripped or a signature-free file, and they are not a pixel-only test. A separate signature-free Midjourney-style file was not tested. SynthID appeared in the final panel only for the ChatGPT example tile.

During the real-photo checks, the page text also contained "Pixel analysis is unavailable right now. Try again" while the visible panel already showed pixel detection "No AI found". That line looked hidden or stale. No retry was needed, because a full verdict was on screen.

The result flow said that images are never stored, and that the check is free with no sign-up. No paywall appeared. The Image details expander was not opened. The Gemini example tile was not run. No feedback thumbs were submitted.

The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is the same set of files, a real photo, a signature-free AI image, a file with a SynthID watermark, and a photo that was only partly edited with AI, in each detector named here, scored on the verdict, whether pixel detection ran, and the published price.
