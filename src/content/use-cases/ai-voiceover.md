## Hands-on test (11 Nov 2026)

This note is for Quillo's read-aloud, at [quillo.tr/text-to-speech](https://quillo.tr/text-to-speech). No account, no password, no Google sign-in and no payment were used. It is a free read-aloud for listening to text. Signed out, it is not a voiceover export: the page uses device voices and does not offer an MP3 download. ElevenLabs and Descript were not part of it.

The text was: "Hollow Oak Bakery opens at 7:30 on 14/03. Call 020 7946 0018; sourdough is £4.50." The check ran from 10:59:28 to 11:00:23 UTC on 11 Nov 2026.

Signed-out read-aloud worked. There was no blocking wall. The first Listen opened the reader in 1.105 seconds and did not start playback. The reader's Listen was pressed at 10:59:59.412 UTC. Pause appeared within 0.566 seconds. Sentence 1 was highlighted yellow. The counter moved from 1/2 to 2/2 by 11:00:09. Audio could not be heard in the test environment, so playback is confirmed from the player UI only. Pronunciation of the time, date, phone number and price is unverified.

Signed out, the voice list was device voices only: Automatic, Google UK English Female, Google UK English Male and Google US English. No lock icons were shown. The reader said: "Read with your device voice. Create a free account for natural Quillo voices." Speeds were 0.8, 0.9, 1, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75 and 2x. 1.25x was selected. The page says device-voice reading has no length limit, and that MP3 download is not available on this page.

The PDF tools page lists merge, split, edit, rotate, delete pages, page numbers, compress, PDF to and from Word, PDF to and from JPG, PNG, photo, EPUB and TXT to PDF, sign, protect, unlock, watermark, listen, and text-to-speech. The merge page opened its file picker with no account. No file was uploaded. The listen-to-PDF page opened with no account. It advertises 1 minute of natural voice, then device voice, and says an account adds 10 minutes. That page was not playback-tested.

Prices seen on 11 Nov 2026, at [quillo.tr/#fiyat](https://quillo.tr/#fiyat): Free is $0, for device voices. A one-time sign-in trial is 10 cloud minutes and 10 AI pages, with no card. Starter is $4.99/mo, with 30 cloud minutes and 15 AI pages a month, or $34.99/yr, with 20 minutes and 10 pages a month. Pro is $9.99/mo, with 90 minutes and 40 pages a month, or $69.99/yr, with 60 minutes and 25 pages a month. These are US prices. Purchases are via mobile app stores. Natural voices need an account. That account was not created, and those voices were not tested.

ElevenLabs and Descript were not part of this check. The standard task on this page has not been run. That task is the same 150-word explainer script, a stock voice and a cloned voice, a blind listen for naturalness and pronunciation of tech terms, and a record of the credits used.
