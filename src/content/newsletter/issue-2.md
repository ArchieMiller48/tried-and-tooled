<h2>Featured stack: AI video for B-roll, ads and shorts</h2>

<p><strong>The job:</strong> get a shot you can't film (a product in a dream setting, an establishing shot, a hook for a short) without burning credits on trial and error.</p>

<div class="callout">
<ol>
<li><p><strong>Shot list:</strong> ask your LLM of choice to turn the idea into 3 to 6 short shots, each with a subject, setting, camera move and length.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Generate:</strong> Runway, or Google Flow on a budget. Use a reference image to keep the product or character consistent.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Sound:</strong> ElevenLabs for voiceover or Suno for a music bed (see our new <a href="https://archiemiller48.github.io/tried-and-tooled/use-cases/ai-music-for-videos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AI music page</a>).</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Edit:</strong> Descript or your usual editor for cuts, captions and the right aspect ratio.</p></li>
</ol>
</div>

<ul>
<li><p><strong>Top pick: Runway.</strong> One subscription covers several video models, so you can switch per shot. Standard is $12/mo billed annually ($15 monthly) for 625 credits/mo, and Free is a one-time 125 credits (checked 19 Oct). <a href="https://runway.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source</a></p></li>
<li><p><strong>Budget pick: Google Flow (Veo 3.1).</strong> Everyone gets 50 Flow credits a day, enough to try a few short clips for £0. Without a paid Google AI plan you may not be able to generate at peak times. Google AI Pro is $19.99/mo (US price, checked 19 Oct). <a href="https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source</a></p></li>
<li><p><strong>Watch out:</strong> Kling's own plan guide says content made on its free Basic plan is not for commercial use. If a clip is going into an ad, a sponsored video or client work, use a paid plan. <a href="https://kling.ai/blog/kling-video-3-0-credit-cost-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source</a></p></li>
</ul>

<p><em>Status: pending hands-on test.</em> Our planned test is the same 3-shot product B-roll sequence in each tool, scored on prompt adherence, product consistency and artefacts, with the cost per usable second.</p>

<h2>One practical tip</h2>

<p><strong>Put a ceiling on your automations before an AI step loops.</strong> If you go over your Zapier task limit with pay-per-task switched on, Zapier bills the extra tasks at 2.5x your base rate on monthly self-serve plans (1.25x on annual), and only stops at 3x your subscription (<a href="https://zapier.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">source</a>, checked 19 Oct). Choose annual billing, turn on flood protection, or switch pay-per-task off, and keep a human approval step on anything that emails a customer or moves money. Running lots of multi-step AI workflows? n8n bills per full workflow run rather than per step (<a href="https://n8n.io/pricing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">source</a>).</p>
