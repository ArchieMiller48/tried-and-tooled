<h2>Featured stack: automate small-business admin</h2>

<p><strong>The job:</strong> new orders or leads arrive, and you keep copying them into a sheet and writing the same confirmation email by hand.</p>

<div class="callout">
<ol>
<li><p><strong>Map it on paper first:</strong> the trigger, each step, and where a human has to sign off.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Trigger:</strong> your form, inbox, store or CRM starts the workflow.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>AI step (optional):</strong> classify the request or draft the reply with AI by Zapier or n8n's AI Agent node.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Human check:</strong> anything that takes money, publishes, or emails a customer gets an approval step or lands as a draft.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Monitor:</strong> turn on failure alerts and cap usage so a loop can't run up a bill.</p></li>
</ol>
</div>

<ul>
<li><p><strong>Top pick: Zapier</strong>, from $19.99/mo on Professional; Free is $0 for 100 tasks a month (checked 16 Nov, <a href="https://zapier.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a>). <strong>Budget pick: n8n</strong>, Cloud Starter €20/mo billed annually, or self-host the free Community Edition (checked 16 Nov, <a href="https://n8n.io/pricing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a>). <strong>Avoid for this job: Zapier Free for anything over two steps.</strong> The Free card lists two-step Zaps only, and a lead, an AI draft and a send is already three.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>What we actually saw with n8n (18 Nov):</strong> self-hosted Community Edition 2.48.5, no account, no card, run from the command line. The install took about 75 seconds and 2.9 GB of disk. Our 9-node bakery order workflow ran in 0.80 seconds: 6 of 6 orders routed correctly, 6 of 6 totals were right, and 4 of 4 confirmation emails were correct. We wrote the rules and email wording ourselves, and no AI was involved. We didn't test the editor UI, n8n Cloud, AI nodes or app integrations, and Zapier wasn't tested, so the page stays marked <em>pending hands-on test</em>.</p></li>
</ul>

<h2>One practical tip</h2>

<p><strong>Test an automation with fake data that hits every branch before you point it at real customers.</strong> For our n8n check we wrote six made-up bakery orders on purpose: four normal ones, one with a blank email and one asking for delivery (the bakery is collection only), so both routes in the workflow had to fire. Checking six rows by hand took minutes. Finding a wrong route after a real customer gets the wrong email takes much longer.</p>
