<h2>Featured stack: proofread and edit</h2>

<p><strong>The job:</strong> catch typos and agreement slips in a client email before send.</p>

<div class="callout">
<ol>
<li><p><strong>Draft offline.</strong></p></li>
<li><p><strong>LanguageTool Basic free pass.</strong></p></li>
<li><p><strong>Fix clear errors.</strong></p></li>
<li><p><strong>Read once yourself.</strong></p></li>
</ol>
</div>

<p><strong>What we saw (4 Nov):</strong> on LanguageTool Basic, signed out, 8 planted errors were all caught at least partly. It missed the has agreement in "Me and the team has". There was 1 arguable false positive. The counter read Characters 246/2000. No Premium locks. The page stays pending.</p>

<h2>One practical tip</h2>

<p><strong>On a free grammar checker, watch the character counter before pasting a long draft.</strong> The LanguageTool homepage editor we used capped at 2,000 characters.</p>
