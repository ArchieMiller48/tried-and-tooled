summary: "n8n's 10 November post releases Nod as a research preview from n8n Labs. It is a separate app from n8n. You do not need an n8n account. It runs on your computer, with its own browser, and works in apps you connect, such as Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Slack, and on websites. The post says it is open source under Apache 2.0 and free. You pay the model provider you connect, or you run a model on your own computer. Before it sends, pays, posts or deletes something you did not ask for, a separate check confirms you asked. The post says that check is itself a model and can be wrong. On a laptop, Nod stops when the laptop sleeps. It cannot make phone calls. Downloads are for Mac and Windows."
